What restarts

No container restart. Saving restarts the adapters in-process and sends a bridge resync.

Restart-gated sections cannot be changed here: [web], [mqtt], [home_assistant], tv.wake and tv.off are rejected at validation.

What the resync does

Choregos re-reads the bus: it asks each role for its power status, then warms for up to 3 s before it acts on power again. The resync itself wakes and sleeps nothing.
